MISSION BRIEFING

Survive the asteroid field for 60 seconds until the Warp Gate opens, then ride the warp stream to safety.

  1. 1. Press ↑ / W to thrust up, ↓ / S to thrust down.
  2. 2. Every move burns fuel. If your fuel hits zero, your ship is stranded — game over.
  3. 3. Dodge all asteroids. One hit destroys your ship.
  4. 4. Difficulty and asteroid speed increase every 10 seconds.
  5. 5. Score points for every asteroid you pass. Higher levels earn a score multiplier.
